# Maintenance guidance seed

## Guidance snippets

### G1 - Compressor bearing degradation response

When a high-criticality compressor shows bearing degradation symptoms, the maintenance response should verify vibration evidence, inspect bearing condition, prepare required spare parts, and plan a controlled maintenance intervention if the production window allows it.

### G2 - Short time-to-failure horizon

When the estimated time to failure is short and the asset supports a production-critical process, the decision should avoid uncontrolled shutdowns. The preferred response is controlled inspection, maintenance preparation, and escalation to human review when the asset is critical.

### G3 - Evidence reliability and operational context

A maintenance recommendation should consider model confidence, sensor trend confirmation, maintenance history, operational dependency, spare-part availability, technician availability, and production load before deciding whether to monitor, inspect, intervene, or defer.

### G4 - Governance for high-criticality assets

For high-criticality assets, recommendations that imply intervention, continued operation under risk, or relevant production impact should be reviewed by an accountable human decision owner before execution.
